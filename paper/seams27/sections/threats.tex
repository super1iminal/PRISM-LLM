\section{Threats to validity}\label{sec:threats}

\paragraph*{Vocabulary}
The domain provides the features, and they carry much of the generalisation: with the shortest-path feature the
rules do best, and without it fewer instances are certified. We report both, give the baselines the same features,
and keep the vocabulary fixed across methods.

\paragraph*{Families we designed}
The gridworld families are ours. We fixed them before running the LLM on them, checked that their thresholds are
achievable and that a hand-written controller fails on most members, and report the random grids and the external
Pac-Man benchmark as well.

\paragraph*{Variance and selection}
LLM runs vary; we report five repeats with ranges and keep a sealed test set that no design decision saw. The
validation grids were used to choose among conditions.

\paragraph*{Baselines}
1--2--3--Go! is reimplemented (CART instead of its tree learner, value iteration instead of Storm); its published
Pac-Man value is quoted, not rerun. PPO's reward and budget were set once and not tuned per family.
